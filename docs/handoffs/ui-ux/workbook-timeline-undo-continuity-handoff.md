## Final interaction policy

- Each admitted Undo, Retry Undo, Inspector mention action and entity-creation link starts an operation-keyed presentation intent at its current invoker and original semantic source. It captures surface, Inspector context, authority generation and a viewport-continuity token. Retry is admitted through the action hook after checking the exact disclosure and uncertain operation; the owner replays the same frozen attempt.
- Undo and Retry Undo stay mounted and focusable while pending. `aria-busy` and `aria-disabled` announce state; handlers guard unavailable or duplicate activation. Tab and Shift+Tab remain native keyboard navigation.
- Pointer, keyboard, wheel, focus, input, composition, scope, authority and Inspector changes retire obsolete restoration. The viewport controller carries an abort signal through focus acknowledgement and geometry/reveal work. The write continues after presentation cancellation.
- An accepted Undo removes only its disclosure. If its gesture still owns focus, receipt removal starts semantic source-row restoration; projection settlement or terminal refresh failure finishes the bounded continuation. The existing visible cell, grid and shell fallback applies, with only required minimal reveal. A newer interaction cannot revive the old token, including after focus returns to `body`.
- Inspector actions and creation retain their existing projection-completion timing under the same focus fence. Failed refresh after an accepted write stays read recovery; rejection and uncertainty retain the disclosure and permitted owner recovery. Mention/version/target and authorization admission checks remain in place.

Retired paths: the `completionFocus` body heuristic, native disabling of pending Undo, removal of pending Retry, and the notice leaf's direct `owner.replay` callback. The exact disclosure matcher is shared by the leaf and Retry admission. Compatibility impact is confined to presentation focus and button accessibility; request route/body/transaction identity, receipt handling, backend contracts and stored data are unchanged. The principal remaining implementation risk is a future asynchronous focus adapter ignoring its abort signal; the controller's current adapter acknowledgement, interruption and delayed-reveal tests cover this boundary.
